F1 Roulette

Players earn Bucks based on their drivers' finishes relative to each driver's rank (handicapped finish position = Finish - Rank + 4) and follows the F1 points scheme, using the driver's handicapped finish.

Pre-Race Rank is the driver's position in the F1 Drivers' Standings at the start of the GP.

Example: Driver is 10th in standings and finishes the race 8th. Handicapped finish = 8 - 10 + 4 = 2 (P2 in F1 speak). F1 awards 18 points for a P2, so you get 18 Bucks.

Handicapped finishes cannot be better than P1 or worse than P22. A driver must finish the race to earn Bucks. DNS/DNF = 0 bucks.
